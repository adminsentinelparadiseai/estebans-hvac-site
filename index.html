Questions

FAQ

How fast can you come out?

Routine visits are usually scheduled within a few days. For no-heat or no-AC situations, call or text and we'll fit you in as soon as possible.

Do you charge for a diagnosis?

There is a service-call fee for the diagnostic visit. You'll get a written quote for any repair before work begins.

Repair or replace?

If your system is over 12‚Äì15 years old, needs major parts or keeps breaking down, replacement can cost less over time. We'll show you both options honestly.

How often should I service my system?

Once a year at minimum. Twice a year (cooling in spring, heating in fall) is best for SoCal homes with both AC and heat.

Do you work on commercial units?

Yes, small businesses and light commercial rooftop and split systems.
